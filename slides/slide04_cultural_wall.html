03 · Cultural Wall

Tunisia's specific challenge

📚
Bilingual education
Arabic + French scripts require separate phonological models — no validated bilingual screening tool exists in Tunisia.
🏫
No screening infrastructure
Neurodevelopmental assessment centers are scarce. Most regions have zero pediatric specialists trained in dyslexia detection.
🚫
Stigma delays help
Cultural stigma around learning disabilities causes families to delay or avoid professional consultation, worsening outcomes.
💡
B.Lexia addresses this gap
Browser-based, hardware-free pre-screening — accessible from any school or home with a standard webcam and internet connection.
