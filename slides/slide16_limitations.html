15 · Limitations & Future Work

Honest scope — limits & next steps

⚠️ Current Limitations

Proxy datasets

Models trained on dyslexia-adjacent data (stuttering, gaze), not Tunisian children directly.

Pre-screening only

Not a clinical diagnosis — flags risk, never replaces a certified specialist.

No Arabic-script gaze data

ETDD70 is Latin-script; right-to-left reading patterns remain to be modelled.

🚀 Future Perspectives

Local pilot studies

Partnerships with Tunisian schools to collect bilingual gold-standard data.

Gamified screening

Child-friendly mini-games to reduce fatigue and improve compliance.

Mobile + offline mode

Progressive Web App for low-connectivity rural deployment.

Arabic & RTL language support

Extend gaze models and UI layout for right-to-left reading patterns in MENA region.

Federated learning

Privacy-preserving model improvement across schools without sharing raw patient data.
